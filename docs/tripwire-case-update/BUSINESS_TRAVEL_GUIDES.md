# Practical business-trip guide seeds

Research checked 20 October 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). These are limited source-backed starting points for product content, not a legally reviewed determination for a specific traveler. Original Vietnamese text is summarized in English; translations here are not certified. Validate current route/form/issuing-office requirements before any real filing. Countries below are useful initial examples, not a verified popularity ranking.

## Vietnam: select the correct track before requesting documents

The official immigration-law summary distinguishes DN1 (business with a legally established Vietnamese enterprise/organization), DN2 (offering services, establishing commercial presence, or relevant treaty activities), and LD1/LD2 work categories [V1]. DN1 and DN2 are not interchangeable and are not blanket employment permission. A short meeting trip and a six-month hands-on engineering placement need different assessment. An e-visa is a separate route to assess for the actual visit, not automatically another label for DN1/DN2.

Product demonstration: add a separate Casey Chen 10-day meeting trip with a Vietnamese host and proposed DN1 route, pending case confirmation. Do not overwrite Casey's existing six-month work assignment or assume every user needs the DN1 route. Nationality, current permission, activities, host and intended dates remain intake inputs.

### Sponsored business route: proposed user journey

1. Confirm the activity, host, traveler and intended visa collection route. Track the suggested category separately from a confirmed category.
2. Check existing employee records and ask only for missing information.
3. Coordinate sponsor authorization/application and any required company setup.
4. Assemble the traveler's application dossier for the selected issuing office.
5. Applicant/authorized sponsor submits through the actual official channel, retaining a receipt/reference.
6. Record the authority result, verify travel-document details and validity, and prepare a departure checklist.

This is a TripWire workflow design based on the official processes below, not a guarantee that every case follows an identical sequence.

### Traveler-side checklist baseline [V2]

The Embassy's in-person/mail page lists a visa application, passport (original or relevant copy depending on issuance method), photographs, applicable approval/supporting material, payment and a return envelope if mailing. The page states six months' passport validity before proposed entry and route-dependent photo requirements. The UI should present the selected route's specifics; do not copy passport/portrait upload instructions from one route into another.

### Host-side checklist baseline [V3, V4]

The sponsor procedure describes organizational setup evidence and an entry-approval request (NA2), with official online/account/signature requirements for the electronic route. Create a Company authorization/setup item only where relevant, a Sponsor request preparation step, a signed/submitted request record and a returned approval record. Host invitation/contact information is tracked separately from government approval. TripWire can reuse stored company facts and prepare a field summary; it cannot supply a company's real legal signature or government account.

The Ministry announced updated forms including NA1, NA1a and NA2 under Circular 70/2026, effective 1 July 2026 [V4]. Do not ship an old downloaded form as the verified current form. If an exact latest template cannot be obtained and checked, export a TripWire preparation worksheet and link to the official portal instead.

### Cost and timing presentation

The 2026 government fee announcement lists a USD25 single-entry visa fee and USD50 for a multiple-entry category under 90 days [V5]. These are schedule references, not a confirmed all-in consular quote. Separate consular/route-specific charges, service fees, courier and payment costs; label unverified totals Confirm with issuing office. Avoid the boundary case of exactly 90 days when applying the announcement's multiple-entry category wording. Its effective date is 1 April 2026.

The sponsor portal describes a five-working-day processing standard for the foreign-mission collection branch after a complete dossier [V3]. This is not total lead time or guaranteed approval. Leave office-specific issuance time as confirm-current rather than combining potentially stale Embassy estimates with sponsor processing into a promised departure date.

An internal T-21/T-14 preparation plan, if used, is company policy chosen for the demo, not a statutory deadline. Show processing estimates, action targets and permission validity separately.

### E-visa comparison [V6]

The official portal describes up to 90 days and fees of USD25 single entry / USD50 multiple entry. Route suitability and permitted activities still need checking; do not assume it grants work authorization or attach sponsor-route forms by default. This pack uses the sponsored business path for the main case illustration and keeps e-visa guidance as a separate route card.

## United Kingdom: business visit, ETA or visitor visa

GOV.UK identifies activities such as meetings and contract negotiation as permitted visitor activities, with other work requiring the appropriate route [U1]. Nationality/current status determines whether ETA, a visa or an exemption applies. Do not generate a Standard Visitor visa application for every traveler.

### ETA example: U.S. employee visiting for meetings

For a traveler confirmed as eligible, prepare the traveling passport, email, payment method and face/passport photos for the chosen application method. GOV.UK permits applying for another person, with different app/online conditions depending on whether they are present [U2]. A company can coordinate collection and a draft summary; do not assume the employee's declarations or live face checks can be completed automatically.

GOV.UK states a GBP20 fee, a decision usually within a day but potentially three working days, and passport-linked validity for two years or until passport expiry, whichever comes first [U2]. These are source-checked estimates/conditions, not guarantees. Approval does not itself guarantee entry [U3]. An ETA is linked electronically to the passport; avoid requiring a visa-sticker upload to complete an ETA case.

### Visa-required business visitor example

Where a visa is required, the Standard Visitor page lists GBP135 for the standard six-month route, online application before travel and a visa-centre appointment [U4]. Evidence guidance supports showing visit purpose, personal circumstances, funding and invitation/employer information as appropriate [U5]. Separate required identity/application actions from suggested/conditional supporting evidence. Do not demand every document on the general guidance page.

## Article and case content contract

Use consistent structured data for each route: jurisdiction, traveler scope, allowed-purpose description, required/conditional/recommended item type, step, assigned party, dependencies, fee currency/scope, estimate versus confirmed cost, official URL, research date, effective date where known and next review state. Do not fabricate exact fees or legal dates for unsupported situations.

The source facts above should become concise article sections and plain-language drawers. The primary work screen should show a clear action rather than all source metadata. A route needs review only where facts or current authority requirements remain unresolved; no generic unassigned adviser row should masquerade as a document.

Suggested footer: TripWire provides administrative tools and general information. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. Requirements and fees can change; confirm your circumstances with the relevant authority or a qualified adviser.
